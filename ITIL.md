**# ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library)**

* A globally recognized framework of best practices for IT Service Management. 
* Its purpose is to ensure that IT services are delivered efficiently, consistently, and in alignment with business requirements. 
* It defines standardized processes such as Incident Management, Problem Management, Change Management, and Service Request Management. 
* ITIL V3 introduced the concept of the Service Lifecycle, which consists of ***Service Strategy, Service Design, Service Transition, Service Operation, and Continual Service Improvement***. 
* Although ITIL 4 is the latest version, many organizations still use ITIL V3 concepts in their daily IT operations





**# Simple to Understand:**

ITIL is a framework of best practices for managing IT services efficiently, ensuring that IT supports business objectives through standardized processes.





**# What Problem Does ITIL Solve?**

ITIL helps organizations answer questions like:

1. How should incidents be handled?

2\. How should changes be implemented safely?

3\. How do we minimize downtime?

4\. How do we improve service quality?

5\. How should IT support business goals?





**# ITIL V3:**
