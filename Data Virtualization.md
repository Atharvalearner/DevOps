A modern data management approach that creates a unified, **logical access layer**, **allowing users to analyze, query, and manipulate data** from multiple, **heterogeneous sources** in real time without physically moving or copying it.



It Involves : **abstracting, transforming, federating and delivering data from disparate sources**

Main goal : To **provide a single point of access** to the data by aggregating it from a wide range of data sources.



Benefits :

1. Abstraction of technical aspects of **stored data like APIs, Language, Location, Storage structure.**

2\. Combine the data result sets across multiple sources (also known as **data federation**)

3\. Provides an ability to deliver the data as requested by users
