- Scaling means increasing or decreasing the capacity of your system (servers, computing power, storage, etc.) based on load or demand.
- Scaling helps you handle traffic smoothly

Types of scaling
1. Vertical Scaling : Add or remove resources on machine
	Advantages : 	1. Simpler to implement
			2. Better for monolithic applications
			3. Easier to maintainance
	Disadvantages : 1. Hardware limitations
			2. Downtime during upgrades
			3. Single point of failure
			4. Not suitable for massive growth


2. Horizontal Scaling : You add more servers in the cluster instead of increasing the size of one server.
   eg. Add more instances/servers, Add a load balancer, Share traffic across servers
	Advantages : 	1. Virtually Unlimited Scalability
			2. Increased Reliability & High Availability
			3. Zero or Minimal Downtime
			4. Automatic Scaling
			5. Better Performance Under High Load
	Disadvantages : 1. More Complex Architecture
			2. More Monitoring Needed
			3. Cost Increases Exponentially
