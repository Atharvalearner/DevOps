***# Agile:***

a software development methodology that focuses on iterative development, continuous customer collaboration, and frequent delivery of working software. Instead of completing the entire project before receiving feedback, Agile divides the work into small iterations, allowing teams to adapt quickly to changing requirements. 



***# Scrum:***

one of the most widely used Agile frameworks. In Scrum, work is organized into fixed-length Sprints, typically lasting one to four weeks. 



The Product Owner prioritizes requirements in the Product Backlog, the Development Team implements the selected work during the Sprint, and the Scrum Master facilitates the process and removes obstacles. 



Scrum also includes events such as Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective to ensure transparency, collaboration, and continuous improvement. 



This approach helps teams deliver high-quality software more quickly while responding effectively to customer feedback and changing business needs.
